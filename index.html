About

Digital work with a darker edge.

I’m Zerginox—a University of Arizona graduate with a bachelor’s degree in IT Management, currently pursuing an MSCS through Penn State. I have earned certification in entry-level Python programming and have hands-on development experience with Python, Kotlin, and Java.

Heavy music and video games are major creative influences on my work. Distant, Lorna Shore, Knocked Loose, DevilDriver, and Malevolence—along with DOOM: The Dark Ages and Elden Ring—help shape the dark, aggressive, and atmospheric aesthetic behind Zerginox.

I’m currently accepting select web design and development projects at no cost as I continue building my professional portfolio.
