WELCOME TO BLACKWATER

Just one more cast.

This lake has 25 unusual residents. A few are easy to meet. Some would rather remain a rumor.

  1. Choose your bait. Tap the bait box. Certain fish will only bite on a specific bait.
  2. Flick upward. Swipe up over the lake or rod to cast. A stronger flick sends your line farther. You can also use Cast Line.
  3. Watch the bobber. After a few seconds it will dip. Tap the lake or Reel It In before the fish escapes.
  4. Fill your journal. Catches and size records are saved on this device. Inspect the journal for bait, times, and lake conditions.
The lake keeps real time.

The clock follows your device's local time. Night is 19:00–06:00. Noon Mirage appears only from 12:00 to 12:05; Witchlight from 03:03 to 03:08. A time and an event listed together must both be active.

The water changes.

Toxic spills, gasoline fires, storms, and algae blooms happen randomly, last a few minutes, and can bring different fish. Reopening the app does not reroll an event.

No bait purchases. No waiting energy. Just a rod, a strange lake, and whatever is down there. Sound is off until you turn it on.
